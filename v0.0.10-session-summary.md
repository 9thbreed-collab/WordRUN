## Comprehensive Session Summary

### From Dev Director's Notes:

**Core Vision & Intent:** WordRun is envisioned as a mobile word-association puzzle game built with Phaser 3, Vite, TypeScript, and Supabase. The game blends word-chain mechanics (similar to Chain Link) with Candy Crush-style progression, power-ups, and multiple game modes. The game features 3,000 levels, organized into 120 "lands" of 25 levels each, each level requiring players to complete chains of 11 associated words.

**Core Creative Principles:** A signature principle is "Story-First Integration," where the narrative is directly infused into gameplay, not merely an add-on. Narrative delivery will occur through various methods, including NPC speech cards, animated cutscenes between levels, and context-sensitive dialogue during gameplay. Gameplay mechanics, word choices, and difficulty will evolve to thematically link with narrative progression, making gameplay a vehicle for storytelling.

**Strategic Priorities (2026-01-06 Reboot):** The project is undergoing a strategic reboot, shifting from technical implementation to a design and market-driven approach. This is guided by "The Four Pillars of Redevelopment":
1.  **Market Research & Positioning:** Understanding competitive landscape, target demographics, and unique value proposition.
2.  **Monetization Strategy:** Designing a sustainable revenue model.
3.  **Story & Lore Integration:** Building narrative depth and emotional connection.
4.  **Design Excellence:** Elevating UI/UX, visuals, and player experience.

**New Development Philosophy:** The adopted philosophy is: Research market → Design for excellence → Build with quality → Launch strategically.

**Visual Appeal & UX Goals:** The art direction aims for a native app feel on par with top-tier mobile games like Candy Crush and Wordscapes. Key technical performance targets include achieving 60 FPS on mid-range mobile devices, an initial bundle size under 2MB, and RAM usage below 150MB through optimization techniques like object pooling and texture atlases. Critical attention is being paid to mobile keyboard UX for typing gameplay and the integration of emotional design principles.
