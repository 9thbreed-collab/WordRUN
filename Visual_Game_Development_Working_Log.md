# Visual Game Development (VGD)

## Working Conversation Log

> This document captures the full conceptual conversation that led to
> the definition and adoption of **Visual Game Development (VGD)** as a
> formal methodology. Purpose: archival, iteration, and future
> public-facing primer extraction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Idea

Visual Game Development is a methodology built around **Playable Early,
Polish Late**.

It prioritizes: - Fast iteration - In-world creation - Visual-first
scaffolding - Human judgment over mechanical perfection - AI as an
accelerator, not a replacement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Motivations

-   Reduce time-to-feedback
-   Avoid multi-year development on ideas that may fail
-   Enable creators to build *inside* the game world
-   Shift effort from tedious setup to quality control and emotional
    design
-   Allow regeneration, repainting, and restructuring without sunk-cost
    paralysis

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Foundational Principle

> Create from inside the world, not from files about the world.

Game engines already make motion cheap. AI makes visuals cheap.
Together, they make *experience* cheap enough to iterate on truthfully.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Visual Game Development (VGD)

### Playable early. Polish late.

VGD treats **playable experience** as the primary unit of progress.

Images, videos, and AI-generated assets are: - Hypotheses -
Scaffolding - Disposable until proven

Craft is not removed --- it is relocated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Creation Loop

1.  Enter the world
2.  Move
3.  Feel friction, delight, confusion, boredom
4.  Change one thing
5.  Re-enter immediately

This loop repeats until clarity emerges.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Stages of VGD

### Stage 0 --- Void

-   Empty space
-   Camera
-   Capsule / pill / hands
-   Motion only

Purpose: establish scale, pace, orientation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 1 --- Visual Hypothesis

-   Generate maps, props, characters, UI via AI
-   Treat outputs as provisional

Question asked: \> Does this move us closer to the feeling?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 2 --- Visual Analysis & Indexing

-   AI observes images via Playwright
-   Slices unique components
-   Generates thumbnails + metadata
-   Everything is named and indexed

Unnamed things are not allowed to survive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 3 --- Placement & Presence

-   Place components live in-game
-   Duplicate, block, remove, reshape
-   Verify by re-entering immediately

This is world sculpting, not file editing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 4 --- Motion & Life

-   Use AI-generated video loops
-   Slice into sprite sheets
-   Use billboards for 3D illusion
-   Lock motion visually, not mathematically

Believability over purity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 5 --- Feedback & Emotion

-   Juice
-   Haptics
-   Sound
-   Timing
-   Anticipation and release

This is where emotional design lives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 6 --- Judgment & Pruning

-   Delete aggressively
-   Collapse variants
-   Remove cleverness
-   Simplify

Nothing is sacred.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Stage 7 --- Deliberate Polish

Only after survival through play: - Repaint - Redraw - Re-animate -
Optimize - Replace scaffolding with craft

Polish is earned, not assumed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Guardrails

1.  Everything must be named
2.  Nothing is sacred
3.  Camera and perspective lock early
4.  Index before expanding
5.  Polish is a phase, not a feeling

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Grid System Caveat

Certain game types require spatial determinism.

To avoid AI ambiguity when modifying scenes: - Use a grid or coordinate
system - Treat grid cells as addressable components - This may be folded
into Stage 2 or added as a structural overlay

This does not contradict VGD --- it stabilizes it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Philosophical Framing

VGD mirrors ordered creation: - Void → motion - Motion → form -
Repetition → refinement - Judgment → rest

Creation becomes embodied, not clerical.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Intent

This methodology is being piloted in a real game. The reference
implementation will inform a future public-facing primer.

Goal: \> Help creators ship this decade's games in this decade.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
